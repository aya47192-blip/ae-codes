The delivered images are project-authored derivatives: tone-balanced/recolored scans, retiled stone/concrete surfaces with drawn grout, granular surface representations, and authored periodic wave/caustic images. Water is an authored bitmap surface, not a photographic claim or dynamic physical water. The underlying pool tile scan is Tiles107. Sea ripple maps are authored from periodic wave fields. No wood materials are used.

Images are embedded in SKP and do not rely on network URLs. Sources were downloaded as images only; no source-repository application/code is bundled or required.
