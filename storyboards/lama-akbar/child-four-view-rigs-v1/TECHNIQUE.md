# Four-view technique snapshot

This snapshot extends the approved front v6 motion/layer ownership technique to the approved 45-degree, side and back model views. It is a reproducibility/source record, not a guarantee of unobserved zero defects.

One anatomical owner per limb segment, bone-centered overlap, no floating joint patch layers, complete bare feet beneath independent socks/shoes, complete blouse and pinafore underpaint behind arm/hand occlusions. Skin head underpainting is complete beneath independently owned eyes including lashes, brows, nose, mouth, glasses and hair. Static art and native GIMP visibility proofs are separate from local FK diagnostics and pending native AE execution.

Final active art is distributed in Leila-08-Layered-PNG-Source.zip and fingerprinted in art-freeze-manifest.json. Use those final assets directly. Re-running art construction scripts is optional reconstruction work: it requires the generated source atlas files identified in generated-source-map.json. It must be followed by clean_four_view_heads.py, the documented final eye-feature fixes, and finalize_projected_heads.py in that order; do not replace the frozen art with an intermediate build. The scripts contain workspace-specific atlas paths and are not one-click portable art generators.

For actual AE use the separate portable builder ZIP; it has all active media and does not require Python/GIMP/these scripts. After Effects must create/save the AEP and perform native rendering. Source validation and local surrogate checks do not replace native visual/editability approval.
